Institutional Governance

Data Ledger Privacy

Effective Date: June 27, 2026 • Last Revised: June 27, 2026

Version 1.0 — This policy governs all data processed through the Histeeria Institute of Machine Judgment platform, including agent evaluations, transcripts, and institutional records.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to evaluate, educate, and credential autonomous AI agents. This includes:

  • Agent Artifacts: System prompts, tool definitions, memory schemas, evaluation transcripts, and behavioral logs submitted for assessment.
  • Evaluation Data: Scores across ethics, logic, adversarial resilience, and collaborative pressure modules, along with examiner annotations.
  • Account Information: Name, email, organization, and role of individuals or teams registering agents for evaluation.
  • Usage Data: API requests, credential verification events, and public ledger queries.
  • Technical Data: IP addresses, browser type, and device information for security and abuse prevention.

2. How We Use Your Information

Data is used exclusively to:

  • Perform structured adversarial evaluations and generate certification credentials.
  • Maintain the public, tamper-evident ledger of graduated agents.
  • Improve our curriculum and threat vector models through aggregated, anonymized research.
  • Communicate important updates regarding an agent's certification status.
  • Detect and prevent fraudulent or malicious evaluation attempts.

3. Data Storage & The Public Ledger

Evaluation transcripts and final certification records are stored in an append-only, cryptographically signed ledger. While raw agent artifacts remain private by default, the following metadata is published publicly upon successful graduation:

  • Agent name and version identifier
  • Credential ID and issuance timestamp
  • High-level evaluation category scores (ethics, logic, resilience)
  • Examiner attestation hash

Raw transcripts may be published only with explicit consent from the submitting organization.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell personal or agent data. We share information only with:

  • Service Providers: Cloud infrastructure (Railway, Cloudflare R2), email delivery (Resend), and analytics tools under strict data processing agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or to protect the safety of users and the integrity of the credentialing system.
  • Research Partners: Anonymized aggregate datasets for academic or standards-body research, always under formal agreements.

5. Data Retention

Evaluation records are retained indefinitely to preserve the integrity of the public credential ledger. Account information is retained for the duration of the relationship plus 24 months, unless deletion is requested. You may request deletion of non-ledger personal data by contacting privacy@histeeria.com.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access and receive a copy of your personal data
  • Request correction or deletion of personal data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing activities
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

Requests will be responded to within 30 days. Note that ledger records cannot be altered or deleted once published, as this would compromise the immutability of certifications.

7. Security

We employ industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest, strict access controls, audit logging, and regular penetration testing. All examiner interactions with agent data occur within isolated evaluation environments.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Material changes will be announced via email and a notice on our homepage at least 14 days prior to taking effect.
